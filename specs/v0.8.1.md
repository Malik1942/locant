# Locant v0.8.1: a tap under the finger, a sound when it lands

**Scope of this spec:** Trackpad taps for the moments a finger is on the trackpad (the outline moving between elements, Option's levels, the ring), two soft sounds for the moment a capture reaches the clipboard or does not, and two switches in Settings › General. Tagged `v0.8.1`.
**Revision:** Sep 19, 2026, after `v0.8`. Decided by Malik the same day: taps and sound, each where it fits; a tap on every new element, at most one per 80 ms; sounds made for Locant and played as system interface sounds through AudioToolbox; the moments in R59 and R60; the material chosen by ear from three candidate sets; released as 0.8.1, not 0.9.
**Out of scope:** a tap on any click; a sound when the overlay opens, on Esc, or on hover; a separate shutter sound for Snap; per-moment switches, a volume slider, or a tap strength setting (macOS owns strength in Trackpad settings); taps without a finger on the trackpad (only private MultitouchSupport API can do that); new copy on the site.

Read `CLAUDE.md`, then the earlier specs. This spec adds AudioToolbox to the frameworks `CLAUDE.md` allows, for one use: playing Locant's two bundled sounds as system sounds. PRD §6.1 "Borrow, don't brand" still holds: the sounds are Locant's own, but the channel and the off switch are the system's, the way the screenshot shutter's are. PRD §6.1 "Quiet until approached" is why nothing taps or sounds at rest or on its own.

## 4. Requirements

**R59 Trackpad taps.** Through `NSHapticFeedbackManager.defaultPerformer` with `performanceTime: .drawCompleted`, so a tap lands with the frame that draws the change. macOS plays them only on a Force Touch trackpad and only while a finger touches it (AppKit's `NSHapticFeedbackPerformer` documentation); it drops anything else silently, and Locant does not try to detect either.

| Moment | Pattern |
|---|---|
| The outline moves to another element (Point, Text), or to another window (Snap, Cut) | `.alignment` |
| Option steps to the next selection level, including the wrap back to the element | `.levelChange` |
| The ring opens after the hold | `.generic` |
| The pointer on the ring enters another segment, or the center | `.alignment` |

- "Moves" compares the highlighted frame with the last one drawn. The first outline after the overlay opens does not tap: a tap means the outline moved. Moving onto the no-element square does not tap; moving off it onto an element does.
- Option's step sends `.levelChange` only; the frame change it causes is not also an `.alignment`.
- `TapGate` admits a tap when at least 80 ms (`motion.hover`) have passed since the last tap it admitted. A dropped tap does not restart the interval and is never played late, so a fast sweep reads as an even ratchet: not a buzz, not silence. One gate serves every tap.
- Never a tap on a click: Shift-click, the final click, the release on the ring, a click on the ball. The trackpad has already clicked under the finger, and a second tap reads as a double click. None in the color picker (pixel steps would buzz) and none while a frame is dragged or adjusted.

**R60 Sounds.** Two sounds of one family, made for Locant.

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| `landed` | Something reached the clipboard: a Point capture on Return (an element, a set, or a frame), Snap, Text, Color, Cut. It starts with the toast. |
| `missed` | Nothing did: `fail` for any `CaptureError`, "No text found", "No subject found". |

- Silent: the overlay opening, Esc, hovering, clicks, the Option step, the "After #n" iteration toast, "No iterations yet", hints, the update alert.
- Playback: `AudioServicesCreateSystemSoundID` once per file when `Feedback` is made, `AudioServicesPlaySystemSoundWithCompletion` in the same turn as the toast; no file is read at play time. `kAudioServicesPropertyIsUISound` stays at its default, 1, so both sounds are silent while "Play user interface sound effects" is off in System Settings › Sound.
- Each sound: mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit linear PCM in CAF; at most 250 ms including the tail; a 2 ms raised-cosine attack, then exponential decay; `landed` peaks at −18 dBFS ± 1 dB, where Tink peaks at −8.8 and Pop at −11.1 (ffmpeg `volumedetect`, Sep 19, 2026); energy between about 500 Hz and 5 kHz, so nothing booms on laptop speakers or hisses in headphones.
- `landed` is two notes rising a fourth, their onsets 70 ms apart, the second 2 dB softer. `missed` is one note a minor third below `landed`'s first, its upper partials damped faster, its peak 3 dB lower.
- The material is chosen by ear from three sets rendered by `design/sound/render.swift`: Glass (a small glass struck lightly), Wood (a soft marimba bar), Felt (a felt hammer on a string). Malik listens on a private audition page, on speakers and in headphones. Only the chosen pair is bundled. The README records every parameter, so a later change is one number and a re-render.

**R61 Preferences and Settings.** `trackpadTaps: Bool` and `sounds: Bool`, both default `true`, read at the moment of each tap or sound. Settings › General gains a section after the floating ball's:
- Switch "Trackpad taps", detail: "A light tap under your finger when the outline moves to a new element, when the ring opens, and between its segments. Needs a Force Touch trackpad."
- Switch "Sounds", detail: "A soft sound when a capture reaches the clipboard, a lower one when nothing did. Follows Play user interface sound effects in Sound settings." Turning it on plays `landed` once.

The Settings window's one-time growth (0.7.3) makes room for the section. No Help row: the switches say what they do.

## 5. Feel test

Nothing a build can run observes a tap, and the render script measures the sounds but cannot judge them. Malik runs this on the installed build, a finger on the trackpad:

1. Point over a toolbar, sweeping slowly, then fast. Slow: one tap per element. Fast: an even ratchet, never a buzz. Onto empty space: no tap.
2. Option three times: a tap per level, and one on the wrap.
3. Hold the ball: one tap as the ring opens. Circle the ring slowly: one tap per segment and one at the center. Release on Snap: no extra tap.
4. Click an element, type a note, Return: `landed` with the toast. Shift-click two elements, Return: `landed` once.
5. Text over an empty area: `missed`.
6. Trackpad taps off: step 1 gives no taps. Sounds off: step 4 is silent. Sounds on again: `landed` plays once.
7. Sounds on, "Play user interface sound effects" off in System Settings: step 4 is silent. Back on, with the alert volume lowered: step 4 is quieter.

The overlay and the ball are non-activating panels, so Locant is never the active app when it taps. If steps 1 and 3 give no taps while the switch is on, stop: activating Locant would change how the app being pointed at looks, so the fix needs a decision, not a workaround.
