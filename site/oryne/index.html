A Locant story

One night with Oryne

After a usability test, I fixed the details of Oryne, my iPhone app, by pointing at them. Eighteen captures between 22:18 and 23:43, each pasted into Claude Code, and the commits that answered them.

Both screens are those commits rebuilt on October 20, with that day's thoughts.

The setup

Oryne is an iPhone app for catching ideas before they drift away. Its big redesign came from a written spec on October 17.

On October 18, three people tried it, and what tripped them up became a list. That night was for the details: I pointed at each one in the simulator with Locant, typed what was wrong, and pasted the capture into Claude Code.
