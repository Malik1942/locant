Why Locant

      
Tested against a screenshot of the same element, with the same note and the same model. The agent had two copies of the code, and only one was on screen.

    

    

      
0 of 20
Locant runs that edited the wrong copy. With a screenshot: 14 of 20.

      
2× faster
39 seconds to the right file. With a screenshot: 80 seconds.

      
Half the cost
$0.55 a run. With a screenshot: $1.13.

    

    
Tested on Claude Code, graded blind. Read the report.

    
See it on a real tweak pass: One night with Oryne, capture by capture.

Private by design

    

    

      

        
Two permissions, nothing more
Accessibility to see what’s under your cursor. Screen Recording to take its picture.

      

      

        
Nothing leaves your Mac
No telemetry, no accounts. The one request is a daily check for a newer version, and you can turn it off.

      

      

        
Signed and notarized
Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

      

      

        
Quiet
No Dock icon. Nothing pulses, bounces, or waits for you to notice it.
