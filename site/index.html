Private by design

Two permissions, both explained

Accessibility reads what is under your cursor. Screen Recording captures the pixels. There is no third prompt.

Nothing leaves your Mac

No telemetry, no accounts. The one request is a daily check for a newer version, and you can turn it off.

Signed and notarized

Every build is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. A dmg from GitHub, not the App Store.

Quiet

No Dock icon. Nothing pulses, bounces, or waits for you to notice it.

Works with your agent

Paste the capture where you want it. Or connect over MCP in Settings › Agents, say what should change, and the agent fetches the capture itself, image included.
